# Janne Särkelä

Sound artist · Developer · Musician  
Creative software · Interactive art · eLearning

## Tect family — sound design from physics and real places

- [Boomtect](https://github.com/janne-s/Boomtect) — Thunder, blasts and sonic booms.
- [Rumbletect](https://github.com/janne-s/Rumbletect) — Jets, rockets and volcanic rumble.
- [Echotect](https://github.com/janne-s/Echotect) — Maps and terrain into spatial echoes.

## Audio tools

- [GeoBuzz](https://github.com/janne-s/GeoBuzz) — Compose music in geographic space.
- [GeoFlute](https://github.com/janne-s/GeoFlute) — Terrain into playable waveforms.
- [Holotoner](https://github.com/janne-s/Holotoner) — Images into stereo sound.
- [Sound Index](https://github.com/janne-s/sound-index) — Find samples across Ableton Live and Logic Pro projects.

## Interactive experiments

- [Algohol](https://algohol.net) — Program algorithms to compete in simulated arenas.
- [VaxVsBug](https://github.com/janne-s/VaxVsBug) — Explore vaccine and disease risks visually.
